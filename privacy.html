Privacy Policy

Fieldkit · Last updated 28 October 2026

Fieldkit is a photography app made by Grzegorz Pluta (“I”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Fieldkit does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any of your data.

What Fieldkit does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No third-party software development kits (SDKs).
  • No data is sent to me or to anyone else.

Information that stays on your device

Fieldkit uses some features of your iPhone only to make its tools work. Everything listed here stays on your device.

  • Location (only if you allow it). Used to calculate sunrise, sunset, golden hour, blue hour, the Milky Way and moon times, and the sun’s and moon’s positions, and to add a place to the shots you save in the Shot Log. Your last location is remembered on the device so the planners work offline.
  • Camera (only if you allow it). Used live by the Light Meter to measure brightness and by the Sun & Moon camera view to show the sky. No photos or video are taken or saved.
  • Motion sensors and compass. Used by the Sun & Moon camera view to know which way your phone is pointing.
  • Your settings and Shot Log. Your camera profile, filters, preferences and saved shots are stored only on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.
  • Notifications (only if you allow them). Used to tell you when a long-exposure timer ends. They are created on your device.

Maps

When a saved shot has a location, the shot editor shows it on a small map provided by Apple Maps. To draw the map, your device requests map tiles for that area from Apple. This is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; I receive nothing.

Apple services

Like any app, Fieldkit is distributed through the App Store, and Apple may provide me with anonymous, aggregated crash reports and usage statistics if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your iPhone’s settings. This is controlled by you and by Apple.

Children

Fieldkit is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
