Fieldkit

Photography tools for landscape photographers

Fieldkit works out exposure, ND filter times, sharp stars, depth of field, golden hour, the Milky Way and the sun's and moon's paths, all on your iPhone and fully offline.

Common questions

How do I learn how a tool works?
Tap ? at the top of any tool, or open Tools → Guide.

The sun in the camera view is a little off.
That's the compass. Tap Line up with the sun, point the circle at the sun and tap Set. If it's far off, move away from metal and wave the phone in a figure 8.

Golden hour or night-sky times don't appear.
Allow location access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Fieldkit, then tap Update location.

The long-exposure timer doesn't alert me when it ends.
Allow notifications in Settings → Notifications → Fieldkit. The countdown also shows on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and on Apple Watch; if it doesn't, check that Live Activities are on in Settings → Fieldkit.

Does Fieldkit need an internet connection?
No. Everything is calculated on your phone. Only the small map in the Shot Log loads from Apple Maps.
