Made for the field

Knows your camera

Set up your sensor, ISO range, fastest shutter and ND filters once in My Gear. Every tool only offers settings your camera has.

Works without signal

Everything is calculated on your iPhone. Your last location is remembered, so the planners work on a remote ridge.

Timer on your wrist

The long-exposure countdown shows on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and on Apple Watch, with an alert when it's time to close the shutter.

Red night mode

One tap turns the whole app red and dims the screen to protect your night vision.

Private by design

No account, no tracking, no ads. Fieldkit doesn't collect any data: your location, settings and shot log stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
